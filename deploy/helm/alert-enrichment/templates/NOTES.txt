1. Point Alertmanager at this service:

   route:
     receiver: alert-enrichment
     group_by: [alertname, namespace, pod]
     repeat_interval: 4h
   receivers:
     - name: alert-enrichment
       webhook_configs:
         - url: http://{{ include "alert-enrichment.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}/webhook/alertmanager
           send_resolved: true
           max_alerts: {{ .Values.enrichment.maxAlertsPerRequest }}

2. Verify:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "alert-enrichment.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
   curl -s localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/readyz

3. Preview a message without Slack (safe in production too):

   curl -s -X POST localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/webhook/alertmanager?dry_run=true \
     -H 'content-type: application/json' --data @alert.json | jq .messages[0].blocks
